About This Demo

Proof-of-Concept System: This is a demonstration project trained to identify handwriting from three specific writers (Hasnain, Mehdy, and Umair). The system uses machine learning to analyze unique characteristics in handwriting samples. While the model performs well on these specific writers, it is not designed for general handwriting identification. This showcases the potential of AI-powered handwriting analysis technology.
